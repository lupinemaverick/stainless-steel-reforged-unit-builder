A preset must earn its difference. Grip, reach, contact behavior, reload, formation or mounted use should change how you fight. A national name, ornate hilt or better equipment grade does not automatically create another weapon type.

Scope: core SSR geography—Europe, North Africa, western Asia and the steppe. Wider-world candidates are separately flagged. Numbers are proposed weapon bases, not final unit stats or measured historical rankings. The live builder is unchanged.

1050–1250

Fundamental swords, axes, maces, spears, lances, bows and crossbows. Early does not automatically mean crude.

1250–1400

More long-sword/staff-weapon contexts. Concrete halberd dates1375–1400 and around1400 handguns anchor the late part of the band.

1400–1500

Rigid points, hammer/beak weapons, pollaxes, developed halberds, pike formations and mechanically spanned crossbows.

1500–1550

Selective late two-handers, broad spear variants and firearm packages; early pistols near the endpoint.

Worth splitting

  • Hammer-face fatigue pressure versus pick-focused immediate Rend.
  • Balanced longsword versus rigid point-oriented longsword.
  • Fighting spear versus long multi-rank formation pike.
  • General glaive versus hooked bill with eligible Pikebreaker/Stagger interactions.
  • Crossbow power, loading method and mobility packets.
  • Bow doctrine and mounted arrow packages where projectile/animation support works.

Merge unless behavior earns a split

  • Pernach, shestoper and gurz: military mace aliases by actual head.
  • Falchion, messer and sabre: inspect blade/grip/context; no automatic national stat boost.
  • Dane axe versus berdiche when geometry or animation gives no meaningful difference.
  • Ranseur, spetum and corseque when control effects are not verified.
  • Regional greatswords; decoration and names are not new mechanics.
  • Pavise crossbow: a crossbow plus shield/deployment loadout, not a new bow invention.

Read the suggested stats correctly

Handling bonus is proposed+0/+1 on top of soldier competence. It replaces the idea of a separate full legacy weapon Skill baseline in a future revision; it is not already the builder formula. No preset here adds a Defense Skill penalty.

Common equipment and Average soldiers are independent defaults. Equipment Grade adjusts relevant equipment stats; Soldier Quality and archetype training apply separately. Grade and troop identity are not additional physical weapon presets.

Delay uses EDU units and existing controls as provisional anchors; the local header describes tenths of a second. Actual cadence depends on animation. Ranged reload delay remains unspecified. Reach needs actual geometry/skeleton support.

Pikebreaker1 is10% per eligible individual engagement and permanently switches that model for the battle. Batter2 adds permanent cumulative fatigue to the individual model on eligible hits/Armor contact; dodge/parry/Shield block prevent stacks. Native fatigue scale and magnitude still require testing.

Spear_bonus dependencies and native spear/light_spear effects need verification. Do not silently add an infantry Defense Skill penalty or a half-Skill pike rule. AP/Rend and new firearm projectile behavior also need a coherent implementation.

Builder integration

Category → functional Preset → optional Regional model/name, followed by independent Soldier Quality and Equipment Grade. Every profile should have a short “Why this differs” description and an explicit category ID.

Only add presets with known native weapon/projectile/skeleton IDs and supported effects. This catalogue JSON is research data, not a builder project ready to import. No plausible-looking IDs have been invented to fake support.

Historical evidence and its limits

Artifact construction and approximate dates guide the catalogue. They do not establish Attack values, fatigue stacks, kill rates or universal military use. A museum object's date is not a weapon invention date.

Row citations provide context and form evidence, not proof of every alias or suggested adoption band. Conditional entries openly identify weaker regional/dating support. Regional breadth is less securely documented than the European late-medieval core.
